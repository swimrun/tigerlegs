**Feature Branch**: `003-training-month-planner`
**Created**: 2026-11-10
**Status**: Draft
**Input**: Field-testing the training week prototype (spec
[002](../002-training-week-assistant/spec.md)) on 10 November 2026; training

[docs/ROADMAP.md](../../docs/ROADMAP.md); on-device model constraints in

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### Primary User Story

As a swimrunner, I want to plan a month of training from a few high-level
choices — how often I run, swim, do strength and mobility — see the four
weeks, adjust any session by dragging it to another day or by editing its
details, and take the plan to my own calendar, so that the plan fits my life
and I stay in control of it.

The division of labour from spec 002 stays: the athlete is in charge, the
plan is generated by explicit rules from the levers, and the on-device
model only helps with moving sessions (ADR-0010: too small for full plan
generation). Nothing is saved to a server.

### Acceptance Scenarios

1. **Given** the training page, **When** it loads, **Then** it shows four
   weeks (a month) of sessions per day and the athlete can go through the
   weeks one at a time.

2. **Given** the plan levers, **When** the athlete sets run to 3 and swim
   to 2 per week, **Then** every week of the month shows 3 runs and 2
   swims, all time based, spread over the days.

3. **Given** the levers, **When** the athlete ticks *Strength* and chooses
   twice per week, **Then** every week gets two strength sessions; unticked
   means none. The same holds for *Mobility*.

4. **Given** strength or mobility set to three times per week with
   sessions of 30 minutes or more, **When** the levers change, **Then** a
   warning says this is a lot next to endurance training, and daily short
   sessions (up to 10 minutes, e.g. a seven-minute routine) are accepted
   without warning.

5. **Given** a plan with, for example, five runs and one swim per week,
   **When** the levers change, **Then** a suggestion says the mix is
   unbalanced for swimrun and offers a balanced alternative that the
   athlete can accept with one action or ignore.

6. **Given** a generated session, **When** the athlete opens its detail
   screen and edits the description or intervals, **Then** the session is
   marked as *hardcoded*, shows that mark in the week view, and no longer
   changes when the levers change.

7. **Given** a hardcoded session, **When** the athlete presses *Remove
   specific details*, **Then** the session returns to a plain time-based
   session and follows the levers again.

8. **Given** a week, **When** the athlete drags a session to another day,
   **Then** it moves there and the change is listed; the same move is
   possible with the keyboard and with the day selector.

9. **Given** the page has just opened with no changes, **Then** no
   *Confirm new week* button is shown; it appears once there is a change
   (issue #40).

10. **Given** a plan, **When** the athlete chooses *Export to calendar*,
    **Then** the browser downloads an iCalendar (.ics) file with one event
    per session that Google Calendar, Outlook and Apple Calendar can
    import, and nothing is sent to a server.

### Edge Cases

- Levers all at zero: show an empty month and a hint, not an error.
- More sessions per week than days (for example run 5, swim 5, strength 3):
  allow two sessions on one day only where the validation rules allow it,
  and warn otherwise.
- The athlete moves sessions by hand and then changes a lever: see open
  question 1.
- A goal event (A or B race) falls inside the month: its session is
  distance based and is not regenerated by the levers.
- The athlete edits details of a generated session, then changes the
  duration lever: the hardcoded session keeps its own duration.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: The planner MUST show a plan of four consecutive weeks and
  let the athlete step through them.
- **FR-002**: The levers MUST include run and swim (0 to 5 sessions per
  week each) and the opt-in disciplines strength and mobility (off, or
  once, twice or three times per week).
- **FR-003**: Changing a lever MUST regenerate the generated sessions of
  all weeks; hardcoded sessions MUST NOT change.
- **FR-004**: Generated sessions MUST be time based (duration, not
  distance). Distance based sessions are limited to goal events (A and B
  goals).
- **FR-005**: The planner MUST validate the levers and show warnings and
  suggestions without blocking the athlete. At least: strength or mobility
  three times per week with sessions of 30 minutes or more is flagged as a
  lot for an endurance athlete; short daily sessions up to 10 minutes are
  not; a large imbalance between runs and swims is flagged with a
  suggestion to rebalance (thresholds: open question 2).
- **FR-006**: A session detail screen MUST let the athlete edit the
  description and the intervals. Saving an edit MUST mark the session as
  hardcoded, and the week view MUST show that mark.
- **FR-007**: The detail screen MUST offer *Remove specific details*, which
  turns a hardcoded session back into a plain time-based session that
  follows the levers again.
- **FR-008**: The athlete MUST be able to move a session to another day by
  drag and drop, and MUST have a keyboard and screen reader equivalent
  (the day selector stays).
- **FR-009**: *Confirm new week* MUST only be shown when the week has
  unconfirmed changes.
- **FR-010**: The planner MUST export the plan as an iCalendar (.ics) file
  generated in the browser, one event per session, with title, description,
  start and duration. Calendar sync through CalDAV or a subscription feed
  is out of scope until accounts exist (roadmap M9).
- **FR-011**: The planner MUST keep every rule from spec 002 that still
  applies: the on-device model only proposes moves, nothing changes until
  applied, and no schedule data leaves the device.
- **FR-012**: All texts MUST follow the page language (en/nl/fr/de).
- **FR-013**: Sports rest days. The athlete chooses them in one multi-select above the week, or asks the assistant for one, by hand or by
  asking the assistant ("I need a rest day on Wednesday"). Making a day a rest
  day moves its sessions to the best other day, one session at a time; no
  session can be dragged, selected or proposed onto a rest day. A model
  proposal that would put a session on a rest day is left out and says so.
  Rest days are planned by rules in code, so they work without the model.
- **FR-014**: Step by step. A proposal shows the step to apply now. What the
  week would still need afterwards (two sessions on a day, runs on consecutive
  days) is listed as possible next steps and offered, one at a time, as chips
  once the first step is applied.

### Non-Functional Requirements

- **NFR-001**: AI-assisted, not AI-dependent: levers, validation, detail
  editing, drag and drop and export all work without the on-device model.
- **NFR-002**: Plan generation is deterministic and unit tested; the same
  levers always give the same plan.
- **NFR-003**: Drag and drop is accessible: keyboard operable, announced
  to screen readers, respects `prefers-reduced-motion`, and works with
  touch.
- **NFR-004**: Generated calendar files validate against RFC 5545 and
  import into Google Calendar, Outlook and Apple Calendar (manual check
  per release).
- **NFR-005**: The prototype label and the no-real-personal-data rule from
  spec 002 stay until the privacy review in roadmap M9.

### Key Entities

- **PlanLevers**: sessions per week: runs (0–4), swims (0–3), swimruns (0–2), short
  strength sessions (0–3) and short mobility sessions (0–3); and the **sports rest
  days**: any of the seven days of the week on which no session may be planned. A
  sports rest day is a fixed part of the plan, not a gap that happens to be empty,
  and not necessarily a quiet day: it may be a busy work day. The same levers always
  give the same week (NFR-002). A strength or mobility session of at most 20 minutes
  is short and may share a day with one main session.
- **Plan**: four weeks of sessions generated from the levers plus the
  hardcoded sessions the athlete made.
- **TrainingSession** (extends spec 002): discipline, day, duration in
  minutes, description, optional intervals, `basis` (time | distance) and
  `origin` (generated | hardcoded).
- **Intervals**: ordered list of blocks with duration or distance, and an
  intensity.
- **PlanWarning**: rule id, message and an optional suggested fix the
  athlete can accept.
- **CalendarExport**: iCalendar text built from a plan.

## Open Questions

1. When the athlete has moved a generated session by hand and then changes a
   lever, should the moved session stay where it is (as if pinned), be
   regenerated into the new arrangement, or should the athlete be asked?
2. Which thresholds define "unbalanced" between runs and swims, and do they
   depend on the athlete's goal event (for example a mostly-swim course)?
3. Is the 10-minute limit for "short daily strength or mobility" right, and
   should the weekly total of strength and mobility minutes be validated
   instead of the count?
4. How are A and B goals entered: by hand in this feature, or from the
   goal-event model of issue #20 and ADR-0010?
5. Is a subscription feed (webcal) wanted later, which needs a server and
   therefore accounts (roadmap M9), next to the one-off .ics download?
6. Should hardcoded sessions also lock their day, or only their content?
